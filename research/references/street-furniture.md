# Street furniture (reference)

- **Road signs:** British-style design. Red-triangle warnings, round red-ring prohibitions, blue circles for mandatory signs, km/h limits (50 in town). Source: https://www.kenyans.co.ke/news/40106-meaning-all-road-signs-kenya
- **Markings:** white or yellow, continuous or broken. **Yellow kerb markings = no stopping/parking.** Black-and-white chequered kerbs at roundabouts and medians are a common sight (unverified against the NTSA spec; treat as an estimate).
- **Speed bumps:** often unpainted, with faded diagonal stripes on newer ones; rumble strips before them.
- **Kerbs:** precast concrete, 15 cm upstand, chipped.
- **Street lights:** galvanised poles 8 to 10 m, single arm, sodium-orange (older) or white LED (newer).
- **Electricity poles:** treated timber about 9 m, crossarm with ceramic insulators, many sagging service drops, and illegal tap-off "spaghetti" in informal areas.
- **Bus stops:** a steel frame with a mabati or polycarbonate roof and a bench; covered in posters.
- **Drains:** open concrete-lined channels along roads with plank or concrete-slab crossings.
- **Footbridges:** steel truss over highways, with stair ramps and hawkers on them.
- **Bins:** rare. Litter piles and burning heaps at the edges.
