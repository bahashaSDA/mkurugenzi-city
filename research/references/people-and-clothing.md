# People and clothing (reference)

Starting notes; expand with photo study before modelling the NPCs.

- **Skin tones:** a predominantly Black Kenyan population with a wide tonal range; include some Kenyan-Asian and other residents in the CBD.
- **Mama mboga:** kanga or kitenge wrap, apron, headscarf; sits on a low stool behind a table of tomatoes, onions and sukuma wiki.
- **Hustler streetwear:** jeans, sneakers, oversized tees, bucket hats and caps, football jerseys.
- **Office wear (CBD):** suits, lanyards, pencil skirts, polished shoes; askaris (city council guards) in uniform.
- **Boda rider:** reflective vest, jacket, helmet optional, gumboots in the rainy season.
- **Matatu tout/conductor:** cap, jacket, a fistful of folded notes, hangs from the open left door.
- **School:** single-colour sweater (maroon/green/blue/grey), white or blue shirt, tie, grey/navy trousers or skirt, black shoes.
- **Sunday:** church dresses, suits, bright headwraps.
- **Maasai:** red/blue checked shuka, beaded jewellery (occasional, mostly on the outskirts and at the CBD craft market).
