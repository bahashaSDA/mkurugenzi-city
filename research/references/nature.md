# Nature, climate and light (reference)

- **Location:** Nairobi is at about 1°17′S 36°49′E, altitude about 1,795 m. Days are about 12 h all year. Sunrise is around 06:20 to 06:40 and sunset around 18:30 to 18:50 (it varies only about ±15 min). Twilight is short (about 20 to 25 min).
- **Sun path:** near-vertical at noon. The sun passes slightly north in June and July and slightly south around December. In-game, the sun crosses the sky east to west with a small seasonal tilt.
- **Seasons:** long rains March to May, short rains October to December, cool and grey June to August.
- **October (now):** jacaranda in full purple bloom across the city.
- **Trees:** jacaranda (purple, Oct to Nov), Nandi flame (orange-red flowers), eucalyptus (tall, pale bark), umbrella thorn acacia (A. tortilis), whistling thorn on the savanna, bougainvillea on walls (magenta, orange, white), banana plants in compounds.
- **Birds:** marabou storks roosting in CBD trees, pied crows, pigeons, sacred ibis on lawns.
- **Soil:** red murram off the tarmac; the dust stains everything up to about 40 cm.
- **Sky:** crisp blue mornings, cumulus building in the afternoon, red-dust haze at golden hour.
