# Vehicles (reference)

## Traffic rules
- Kenya **drives on the left**; vehicles are right-hand drive; matatu passenger doors are on the **left**.
- Number plates: format **LLL NNNL** (e.g. "KDA 123A"). Front plate white, rear plate **yellow**, black letters. The KD series is reported exhausted (Aug 2026) and **KEA** plates are now appearing. Electric vehicles (2026): white plates with green characters. In-game: fictional combinations only, avoiding real-looking specific numbers, e.g. "KMK 001M" for Abel's vehicles.
- Sources: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vehicle_registration_plates_of_Kenya · https://tech-ish.com/2026/08/27/kea-number-plates-are-showing-up-on-kenyan-roads/

## Matatu (nganya)
- 14-seater van (about 5.4 x 1.9 x 2.3 m, est) and 25 to 33-seater minibus on a truck chassis (7.5 to 8 m). Nganya bodies are often fabricated from scratch on a stripped truck chassis.
- Style: full-body airbrushed graffiti (celebrities, superheroes, footballers, religious figures, slogans), tinted windows, LED/neon lighting, multiple video screens, big sound systems, chrome rims, a yellow stripe, route number and destination on the front, slogans on the rear windscreen.
- 2025: NTSA ordered graffiti and tints removed and the High Court upheld it; in May 2026 the President ordered NTSA to allow graffiti. Status is in flux, so in-game nganyas are fine as a cultural celebration.
- Mkurugenzi nganya: original livery, "MKURUGENZI" lettering, "UTAFRAAAIA" on the rear windscreen, route "No. 1M", fictional Sacco "WAKURUGENZI SHUTTLE". No real Sacco names and no third-party celebrity faces.
- Sources: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matatu · https://www.kunc.org/npr-news/2026-04-15/greetings-from-nairobi-where-taking-a-matatu-is-no-ordinary-bus-ride · https://www.the-star.co.ke/news/2026-05-22-ruto-orders-ntsa-to-allow-graffiti-on-matatus

## Boda boda
- The dominant model family is a Bajaj Boxer-type commuter (100 to 150 cc), about 2.0 m long, 0.75 m wide, seat height 0.8 m (est). Model it generically with no brand marks.
- Accessories: long passenger seat, crash bars, fluffy seat cover, mirror tassels, mudflap with a slogan, sometimes a box on the rear rack.
- Rider: reflective vest (yellow/orange), helmet is optional (low compliance is documented), spare helmet hanging off the mirror.
- Sources: https://www.bajajauto.com/en-ke/blogs/blog-boxer-bikes-on-kenyan-roads · https://www.howwemadeitinafrica.com/p/can-the-smart-jacket-become-a-competitive-advantage-for-motorcycle-tax

## Private cars and other vehicles
Toyota-style saloons and station wagons (Probox-type estate cars are everywhere), pickups, and old Peugeot-style taxis. All generic and unbranded. Mkokoteni handcart: about 2.4 x 1.2 m, two car-tyre wheels, wooden bed, long shafts.
