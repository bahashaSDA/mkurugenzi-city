# Mkurugenzi City: Style Guide

## Look
Grounded realism: PBR, real scale, dust, rust, peeling paint, wires and clutter. No outlines, no toy look. The Bruno Simon playfulness is limited to specific spots (SPEC section 11).

## Signage rules
- Hand-painted signs in the hub, printed signs in the CBD.
- English/Swahili/Sheng mix.
- No real company, TV, Sacco or telco logos.
